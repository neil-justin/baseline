I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Jobs and Labor Market Forecast, the flagship labor market information report of the Bureau of Local Employment, forecasts the state of the labor market for the next two years by giving information on Key Employment Growth Sectors, Emerging Industries, In-Demand, Hard-to-Fill Occupations, and other relevant information on the labor market. It was mainly sourced from a mix of primary and secondary sources, discussing four main com pon ent s, namely, the demand side, supply side, employment facilitation programs, and development plans. Accordingly, the following findings can be gleaned from this report:

A. Labor Market Context

Various economic and employment indicators show that the labor market is back in its pre-pandemic state. The relaxation of mobility restrictions has likely made education and training more accessible, leading to an expected increase in labor supply. Additionally, as industries recover, there is a projected increase in demand for workers. While external factors may pose challenges to economic growth, the administration of President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. is dee med to be in a favora ble posi tion to implem ent nat ional e mploym ent stra tegies an d contribute to the country’s National Vision, considering the manageable pandemic situation.

B. Labor Market Outlook

Key Employment Growth Sectors are expected to boost employment creation with increased economic activities in the industry, agriculture, and service sectors. The transformative effects of the Digital Economy and Green Economy are anticipated to have a significant impact on the labor market. These sectors are expected to create new job opportunities and drive economic growth. However, to fully benefit from these developments, it is crucial to focus on skills development. Ensuring the availability of a skilled workforce will be essential for individuals to take on job roles in these emerging sectors. By investing in education and training programs that align with the demands of the Digital Economy and Green Economy, the country can better position itself to harness the potential benefits and meet the evolving needs of the labor market.

C. Employment Facilitation Programs

The Department of Labor and Employment continues to improve and update its existing employment facilitation programs to cater the needs of its clientele and to respond to the changing labor market conditions. It has been enhancing its labor market information systems, and continuously improving its job matching system to provide more accurate and relevant information to job seekers and employers. The promotion of skills development training is crucial in ensuring that individuals have the necessary skills to meet the demands of the changing labor market. Additionally, interventions focused on career development support, youth employability, and other employment facilitation mechanisms can further enhance the department’s efforts in addressing the needs of its clientele. These initiatives demonstrate a proactive approach towards adapting to the evolving labor market conditions and supporting the workfo rce in their career journeys.
